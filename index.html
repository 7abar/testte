01 — The thesis

The cheapest data to defend is the data nobody stored.

Most venues ask you to hand over an identity before they will show you a price. Hubhood inverts that: the router only needs to know what you want filled, not who you are. Everything else is noise it was never designed to keep.

02 — How it works

Four steps. Nothing kept afterwards.

A swap through Hubhood is a short-lived job, not a relationship. It opens, it fills, it closes.

  1. 01

    Request

    You state the pair, the size and the destination address. That is the entire input — no email, no profile, no document upload.

  2. 02

    Route

    The router prices your order against aggregated centralised and on-chain depth, then splits it into whichever legs fill best.

  3. 03

    Settle

    Legs execute in parallel and land at the address you named. Funds move through settlement, never into an account you have to trust.

  4. 04

    Discard

    The session closes and the routing context goes with it. There is no profile left behind to link this trade to the next one.

03 — What you get

Built for people who would rather not explain themselves.

Non-custodial by default

Balances are never parked in a house wallet waiting on a withdrawal queue. Assets move from your address to your address.

No accounts, no KYC

Nothing to register, nothing to verify, nothing to lock. Access is a URL and a wallet, the way it was before intermediaries got involved.

Cross-chain reach

One request can cross chains without you juggling three bridges, four approvals and a spreadsheet of intermediate tokens.

Aggregated depth

Centralised order books and on-chain pools are quoted side by side, so size gets filled where it actually costs least.

Execution-aware

Orders are sliced and sequenced to keep intent off the public mempool for as long as possible, and to keep slippage boring.

Minimal retention

The system is designed around collecting as little as possible, because storage is a liability and a subpoena is a query.

06 — FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to make an account?

No. There is no sign-up step, no password and no verification queue. You connect a wallet, state what you want filled, and the router does the rest.

Does Hubhood hold my funds?

No. Hubhood is non-custodial — assets move through settlement to the address you specify. There is no house balance and therefore no withdrawal to request.

What does "private" actually mean here?

It means the system is built to collect as little as it can get away with. Hubhood does not ask for identity, does not build a profile out of your trades, and discards routing context when a session ends. It is not a claim that a public blockchain becomes invisible — on-chain settlement is still on-chain.

Where does $HUB launch?

$HUB launches through Pons. The contract address is published on this page and on @hubhood_xyz at launch — treat every other source as fake until you have checked it against those two.

Is the router live yet?

Not yet. Phase 01 is the site, the community and the token. The beta router follows in Phase 02, with public access in Phase 03.
